Research Assistant Professor · Drexel University
Department of Chemical & Biological Engineering · Philadelphia, PA

I am an experimental physicist and materials scientist (Ph.D., Physics) and a Research Assistant Professor at Drexel University, with 18+ years of analytical laboratory experience. My research connects molecular and mesoscale structure to macroscopic performance across soft matter, polymers, complex fluids, and engineered materials.

I specialize in vibrational spectroscopy (Raman/FTIR), X-ray and neutron scattering, rheology, and thermal/mechanical analysis — applied to ionic liquids, glass-forming systems, peptide and fibrillar materials, nanofibers, gels, foams, additive-manufacturing resins, and active packaging. I translate complex measurements into actionable conclusions for both fundamental science and industry-facing product and process challenges.
